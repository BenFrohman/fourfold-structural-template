\begin{abstract}
Standalone mathematical specification (`README.md`, `INTEGRAL\_HODGE.md`) proving the locked structural equivalence L <-> (Delta\_miss = empty) for candidate cycle-spanning submodules on projective fourfolds, and establishing why L does not imply vanishing of the ambient Hodge defect Delta\_Hdg = empty.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, Scientific Honesty, and Non-Claim Boundary}
Establishes logical boundary lemmas showing why local span closure L neither proves nor disproves the rational Hodge Conjecture.
